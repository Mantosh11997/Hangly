# Creature charm art

Every PNG (or WebP) here ships as a built-in charm in the Creatures
category, named after its file: `king_cobra.png` shows up as "King cobra".

The creature's grip (feet, tail tip, jaws) must touch the top edge,
centred: Hangly ends the cord there and draws no ring on picture charms.
Charms you add with Import Charm… also land in Creatures, and get their
fake checkerboard/white background removed and margins trimmed on import.
Files placed here directly are used as-is, so give them a real transparent
background.

Rebuild the app (or restart `flutter run`) after adding files.
